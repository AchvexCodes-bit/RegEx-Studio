05 · Quick reference

Classes — [a-z], [^0-9]

Quantifiers — * zero or more, + one or more, ? optional, {m,n} bounded.

Alternation — cat|dog; Groups — (ab)+.

Shorthand — \d digit, \w ASCII word, \s whitespace.

Supported automaton syntax is documented in README. Unicode, assertions, backreferences and captures are not compiled by the automaton engine.
